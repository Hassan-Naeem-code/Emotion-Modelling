Two Axes, One Guarantee: every result, checkable

Valence–arousal estimators on AffectNet with five uncertainty methods, five seeds each. Per-axis 90% intervals cover the true VA point only ~86% of the time; a single joint score restores 90% with smaller regions; and an expression absent from calibration breaks every guarantee. Below is every number behind the paper, per method, per seed and per expression.

Accuracy and probabilistic quality

CCC higher is better; RMSE, NLL, CRPS, ECE, AURC lower is better. Ensembles combine the five seeds, so they have one value.

Can anything flag the unseen expression?

AUROC for separating contempt from seen test faces (0.5 = chance), and the share of contempt flagged at a threshold set on validation data so that ~5% of seen faces are flagged. Feature-space detectors use the network's penultimate features.
