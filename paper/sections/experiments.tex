\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Data.} We train and evaluate on the AffectNet valence--arousal
release~\cite{mollahosseini2017affectnet}, using the dataset's own splits:
$58{,}803$ training, $3{,}500$ validation, and $5{,}000$ test images with
continuous VA labels in $[-1,1]^2$. Splits are deterministic and seeded. The
dataset is registration-gated and is not redistributed; a synthetic generator
reproduces the full pipeline for continuous-integration smoke tests but supplies
none of the numbers reported in Section~\ref{sec:results}.

\paragraph{Distribution shift.} Our shift condition is corruption-based, in the
spirit of ImageNet-C~\cite{hendrycks2019benchmarking}: we re-evaluate each trained
model on the AffectNet test set with Gaussian blur, Gaussian noise, brightness,
contrast, and JPEG compression applied at a fixed severity, cycling
deterministically through the five corruption types by image index so the shifted
set is reproducible. Because this perturbs the \emph{same} images and labels, it
isolates the effect of shift on calibration with no confounding from a change of
subjects, annotators, or label protocol --- a confound that a cross-dataset
comparison cannot rule out. A cross-dataset shift to AFEW-VA~\cite{kossaifi2017afew}
runs identically through the same harness where that (registration-gated) data is
available; we do not report it here.

\paragraph{Training.} All models share an ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNet-B0
backbone on $224\times224$ face crops, trained with AdamW (learning rate
$3\times10^{-4}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$), batch size $64$, gradient clipping at
$1.0$, for $30$ epochs. Following our own premise that calibration is the
objective, the checkpoint is selected on validation \emph{interval calibration
error} rather than on validation MSE --- except for the deterministic baseline,
which has no interval to calibrate and is therefore selected on MSE. Baseline,
Gaussian NLL, and evidential are each run at three seeds ($1337$, $7$, $21$).
Split conformal is calibrated on the validation split at $\alpha = 0.1$ and is
reported for a single seed.

\paragraph{Metrics.} \emph{Accuracy:} concordance correlation coefficient (CCC)
for valence and arousal, plus RMSE/MAE. \emph{Calibration:} regression ECE via
quantile calibration, and sharpness (mean predictive interval width).
\emph{Selective prediction:} risk--coverage and accuracy--rejection curves,
summarised by the area under the risk--coverage curve (AURC). \emph{Shift:}
calibration degradation $\Delta$ECE and the AUROC of separating in-distribution
from corrupted inputs by the uncertainty score. \emph{Conformal:} empirical
coverage versus the $1-\alpha$ target on in-distribution and shifted data.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} Every reported value traces to a YAML config, a
seed, and a \texttt{metrics.json} committed under
\texttt{results/summary/real/<method>/seed<n>/}. Table~\ref{tab:comparison} is
emitted from exactly those files by \texttt{eval/aggregate\_seeds.py} and is not
hand-maintained.
